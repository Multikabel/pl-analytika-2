PL Analytika v1.7.3 fallback cross-check hotfix

Fixes: Fallback schedule cross-check rejected all rows.

Cause:
The FPL fallback and fixture schedule use different team identifiers/naming,
so exact (home, away) pair matching rejected valid matches.

- future or unparseable rows are rejected
- the validated 380-match schedule is used only as a maximum-count sanity check
- SQLite pd.NA safety from v1.7.2 is retained
